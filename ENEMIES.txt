Here's a list of enemies you will encounter along with their abilities:

- Alcoholic
	- Throws molotovs at you (can miss)

- Car
	- Moves along a predefined path
	- Deals damage by running you over
	- Cannot be killed and not counted towards enemy kill count
	- Variants include Optimus Prime and Dababy Convertible, which do the same thing but have different hitboxes

- Cluster Strike
	- Sends a horde of rockets onto your location
	- Cannot be killed and not counted towards enemy kill count

- Creeper
	- Runs up to you and explodes (can miss)

- Dublinn Evocator
	- Spawns a fireball that follows you
	- Fireball will despawn after 60 seconds
	- Deals damage if the fireball touches you

- Holy Cow
	- Shoots lasers
	- Cannot shoot through walls, and will only shoot if there is a clear line of sight

- Mafioso
	- Shoots with a pistol
	- Cannot shoot through walls, and will only shoot if there is a clear line of sight

- Magma Cube
	- Can only perform melee attacks
	- Unlike the real magma cube from the popular video game "Minecraft", this one will not split into smaller ones upon death.
	- However there are still 3 size variants which vary in speed and attack strength

- Markiplier
	- Throws hands
	- He's angry he didn't get into the FNAF movie

- Nikocado Avocado
	- Jumps up and down, sending shockwaves

- Pompeii
	- Shoots fireballs that inflict poison (continuously lose HP)

Boss enemies include:

- Osama bin Laden
	- Launches grenades (can miss)
	- Gets enraged (gains attack speed) when HP drops below certain percentage

- Ohio Final Boss
	- Thrusts with sword
	- Made in Ohio: teleports behind you and slashes
	- Ohio Bankai: sword enlarges and performs a wide slash
	- Sigma Stare: long-ranged attack
	- Star Platinum za Ohio: freezes a random quarter of the map, you cannot move if you are inside
	- Wonder of Ohio: crashes a planet into the ground (can miss)

- Faraday

	- Made in Ohio: teleports behind you and slashes

	- Sigma Stare: long-ranged attack

	- Wonder of Ohio: crashes a planet into the ground (can miss)
	- Eddy Current: slows you down
